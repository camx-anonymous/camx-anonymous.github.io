CAMX Dataset / validation clips

Projection overlays · validation you can look at

The gripper drawn back onto its own cameras.

For each dataset family, a few episodes are re-rendered with the gripper's URDF (or, for rigs without a released URDF, the recorded camera path and world-up axis) projected through the dataset's own per-frame camera poses and intrinsics. If the drawn gripper sits on the real one in the wrist views, the end-effector pose, camera extrinsics and intrinsics shipped in the dataset are consistent. Every camera view is stitched side by side in one clip. A few example episodes per project, picked from the validation set; clips stream from the overlays-v1 release.

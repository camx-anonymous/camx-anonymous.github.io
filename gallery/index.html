About coverage and calibration +

These previews use published samples or refreshed local exports, identified with each video. Public sample downloads may predate the refreshed previews. Some previous website examples use other episodes or reviewed data that is not public, so this gallery does not claim to duplicate every old clip. A successful render verifies execution and video length; geometric alignment has not been scored. Known sample-specific limitations are listed with each video.
